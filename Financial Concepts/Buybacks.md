Generally the best way to return capital to shareholders as it is more tax efficient than [[Dividends]] for long term holders. 

Companies spend money to buy back their own shares. This results in fewer total shares which increases the proportional ownership of the company for all of the remaining shares.

Buybacks can be harmful if they are poorly timed. For example if a company buys back lots of shares when it is overvalued, it may have no cash to weather the down turn and may even have to sell shares at low prices causing massive dilution. 

In dying or volatile industries and in riskier jurisdictions, [[Dividends]] are favored as investors would rather get the money now because future valuations of the company could be significantly lower than they are now, and thus buybacks might be ineffective.
