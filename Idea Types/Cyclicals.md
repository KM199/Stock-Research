**Definition**: Companies whose fortunes rise and fall with the economic cycle. **Characteristics**:
- Example industries: automobiles, steel, airlines
- Earnings and stock prices can fluctuate significantly based on economic conditions.
- Can experience periods of rapid growth followed by sharp declines. 
**Investment Approach**:
- Requires timing the market to buy during economic downturns and sell during upswings.
- Analyze industry cycles and economic indicators to predict performance.
- Suitable for investors with a good understanding of economic trends and the patience to wait for the right buying opportunities.
