## Overview
STRC is one of [[MSTR]]'s preferred shares. Designed to maintain a par value of $100 through adjustable monthly dividend payments.

Dividend Adjustment Policy:
1. Minimum dividend is SOFR
2. Maximum month to month reduction of 0.25%
3. Last 5 day VWAP decision tree:
	- vwap > 101 → 0.25% decrease (or more issuance)
	- 99 < vwap < 101 → no change
	- 95 < vwap < 99 → 0.25% increase
	- vwap < 95 → 0.5% increase

Note: only conditions 1 and 2 are contractually obligated. Dividend payments are discretionary but any missed payments accrue and accumulate interest, and there is a dividend stopper provision — all shares junior to STRC cannot be paid until STRC is paid.

## Thesis
- Short term: 95.6 → 100 — MSTR will increase the dividend. Easy month, timeframe 1-3 months.
- Long term: good yield 9.4% right now, can be leveraged. Ideally will be able to sell for 100 at desired risk level.
