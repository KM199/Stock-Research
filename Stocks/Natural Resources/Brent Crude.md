Overview: Global seaborne crude oil benchmark. Traded as ICE Brent Crude Futures (symbol B / BRN), 1,000 bbl/contract, deliverable via EFP with an option to cash settle against the ICE Brent Index. Last trading day = last business day of the 2nd month preceding the delivery month (e.g. Sep26 → last trade 7/31/26).
