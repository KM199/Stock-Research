Idea: [[Cyclicals]]
Industry: [[Ship Lessors]]
Last update: 2024-09-15

## Overview
Star Bulk Carriers owns 159 [[Bulk Carriers]] which they lease out to [[Shipping Operators]]. Star also provides the crews for their ships.

## Thesis

### Shareholder Return Policy
Source: [[SBLK 24-08-12 Interview]]
- **Dividend Policy:** Since 2019, the company pays out all operating cash flow after debt service (interest and principal repayments) as dividends. Maintains a cash reserve of $2.1 million per vessel before declaring dividends. Declared dividends for 14 consecutive quarters, including $0.70 per share for Q2 2024. Total dividends paid approximately $1.25 billion since June 2021.
- **Debt Reduction:** Pays down about $200 million in debt annually. Reducing debt lowers interest expenses and strengthens the balance sheet.
- **Capital from Vessel Sales:** Proceeds from selling vessels are retained rather than distributed as dividends. Used for future investments, additional debt reduction, or potential share buybacks.

### Expansion Plan
Source: [[SBLK 24-08-12 Interview]]
**Objective:** Double market capitalization to approximately $5 billion.
**Reasons:**
- Attract large institutional investors — larger size accommodates substantial investments (~$500 million) from big funds
- Improve market valuation — bigger companies often trade at higher valuations above asset liquidation value
- Competitive advantage — increased scale enhances operational efficiencies and better manages environmental regulations

**Strategies:** Mergers and acquisitions (e.g. recent merger with Eagle Bulk Shipping). Focus on fleet optimization and advanced technologies.
