## Demand for Shipping
* Somewhat unpredictable as it depends on the health of global economy
- Over the long term it should grow at a similar rate to the global economy
- Small risk to long term trajectory from [[Near Shoring]]

## Supply of Shipping
* Generally consistent and predictable, unless their is a supply shock
	* Ships and port infrastructure years to be developed
	* Ships are giant, easy to track, and have lots of data available on them
	* 95% of all ships are made in [[China]], [[Japan]], and [[Korea]]

## Industry Type:
* Highly Cyclical
* Moderately Capital Intensive
	- Can be adjusted through owning ships directly or leasing ships from [[Ship Lessors]]

## Risks:
* [[Global Recession]]
	* This will happen at some point
* [[Near Shoring]] and a potential [[US-China Trade War]]
	* [[Trump]], and the US political class at large want to decouple the US and China, however this is a very difficult task.
	* There would be a short term boost to demand before tariffs go into place as companies rush to import
	* [[Friend Shoring]] is the most likely scenario and this wouldn't impact shipping demand that much
* End of the Russian [[SMO]]
	* This could result in the removal of sanctions but this is unlikely as peace talks have lead to nothing so far, and it's increasingly likely that Russia will not return Ukrainian territory, and thus the west will keep up the sanctions regime. 
* End of the [[Houthi Blockade]]
	* Currently most ships that used to use the Red Sea have to travel all the way around Africa
	* The crisis has no end in sight
