Ship Lessors own ships and lease them to [[Shipping Operators]]

# Industry Type
- Highly Cyclical
	- Downturns for in [[Marine Transportation]] can be extremely dangerous
		- [[Shipping Operators]] usually have a mix of owned and leased vessels. In a downturn, they will cut back on new leases.
		- [[Shipping Operators]] often go bankrupt, leaving unpaid rent and cancelled leases
		- Ship day rates plummet and so do ship values
- Capital Intensive
	- The ocean always wins, ships only last ~25-30 years.
	- New environmental regulations require expensive retrofitting or expensive new ships.
