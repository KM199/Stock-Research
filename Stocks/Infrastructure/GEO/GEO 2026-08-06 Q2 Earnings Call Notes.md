## ICE property sales (the thesis update)
Zoley:
* ICE originally eyed ~10 privately owned turnkey processing centers; now likely more
* CXW sold 4 facilities / ~7,190 beds for >$2.2B at >$300k/bed average
* GEO owns/operates **23** ICE detention facilities vs CXW’s **11** post-sales
* GEO “engaged in an active process for the sale of several” turnkey facilities — sell buildings, keep long-term support-services contracts
* Appraisal basis = replacement cost by geography (same as CXW/DHS)
* No PSA / no timeline / no assurance
* Proceeds → debt paydown, buybacks, G&A (subject to indenture/credit restricted-payment tests first)

Procurement for 4 facilities (CO / WA / FL / PA regions, ~5.5k beds) is a separate track: recompete for new long-term support-services terms; management hopes done by end of Q3, maybe Q4.
