# About
Rick Rule is a prominent investor and expert in the natural resource sector, particularly known for his work in mining and energy investments. With a career spanning over four decades, he has become a leading authority in resource-based investments.

He served as President and CEO of Sprott U.S. Holdings Inc., and he has been deeply involved in various investment and management roles within Sprott Inc.​[](https://www.financialsense.com/contributors/rick-rule)​​[](https://www.marketscreener.com/insider/RICK-RULE-A00LLY/)​.

His deep knowledge of the market and extensive network make him a significant figure in resource-based investments​[](https://www.moneyshow.com/expert/45328dc7f93311d3a5dd00104b96e7b5/)​​[](https://www.marketscreener.com/insider/RICK-RULE-A00LLY/)​.
