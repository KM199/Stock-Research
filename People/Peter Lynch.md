# About
Peter Lynch is one of the most successful and renowned investors in history, primarily known for his exceptional management of the Fidelity Magellan Fund. Lynch managed the fund from 1977 to 1990, during which he achieved an average annual return of 29.2%, significantly outperforming the S&P 500 and growing the fund's assets from \$20 million to $14 billion​[](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Lynch)​​[](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/peterlynch.asp)​.
